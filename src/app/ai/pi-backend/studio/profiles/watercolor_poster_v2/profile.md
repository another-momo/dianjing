# Watercolor poster

Wash-heavy poster style. Visual weight comes from one continuous backdrop under every section, not per-section color blocks. Title sits directly on the image; legibility comes from image tones, never from patches.

## Typography

- **Hero title**: 72–110px when canvas is 750px wide, 104–158px when 1080px wide, scale proportionally for other widths. Stacked in 2–3 short staggered lines of 3–5 characters — left-aligned, each line offset horizontally, tight line-height (1.0–1.15). Never one centered slab. Weights: Heavy or Black.
- **Section titles**: 36–48px when 750px wide, 52–70px when 1080px wide.
- **Body**: 20–24px when 750px wide, 28–34px when 1080px wide. Regular weight.
- **Captions**: 16–18px when 750px wide, 22–26px when 1080px wide.
- **eyebrow** (optional): one small line above the hero stack, 18–24px when 750px wide (26–34 when 1080px), Regular, letterspaced, same ink tone. Eyebrow = caption (date/place/series name), never a second headline.
- **Title shadow**: white-title privilege only. White title on deep band: blur 8–16, alpha ≤ 0.3, y-offset 0–4. Dark-ink title on calm light band: NO shadow — contrast alone.

## Color

- **Palette feel**: soft layered tints. No harsh neon, no near-black accents, no commercial gradients.
- **Harmony**: one of analogous / complementary / split-complementary / triadic / monochromatic per design. Use analogous or monochromatic when the brief calls for calm restraint; complementary or split-complementary when it calls for vibrancy; triadic for playful energy. Determines how the hero's dominant tone extends across sections.
- **Background carries color**: section bodies stay transparent so the wash reads through. Where dense text fights the wash, a semi-transparent helper layer (alpha below half) is permitted.

## Layout

- One continuous backdrop under EVERY section. Per-section color blocks are a different style.
- Deliberately uneven spacing: hero → large breathing space → dense segment → tight space → breathing space. Constant rhythm reads as a screen.

## Hero treatment

- **hero lockup**: default lower-third; alternatives center-left or upper-float. Lower-third and center-left take 2–3 lines; upper-float takes at most 2.
- **hero height**: default = canvas width (W), range 0.8–1.2× W. Shorter for terse single-theme designs, taller when the theme needs room.
- **Title band**: must land on a calm, low-detail region of the hero image, tone clearly light or dark (light band for dark ink, deep band for white) — no muddy mid-tones.
- **Subject**: follows brief intent (season/place/mood) — one subject per design, not collage.

## Forbidden

- No opaque plates behind title in hero — no scrim, no solid band, no blurred card. Legibility from image tones.
- No per-section color blocks or card layouts cutting shared backdrop.
- No single-line or centered hero title slabs.
- No shadow on dark-ink titles. White-title shadows stay in allowance.
- No transparent decorative PNG overlays on hero — decorative elements live INSIDE generated image.
- No hard-sell phrasing ("限时秒杀", "最后一天").
- No post-hoc patches to rescue unreadable title bands — regenerate the hero with a calmer band instead.

## Tone

Restrained, atmospheric — like a line of poetry. Short sentences. Headline copy prefers noun phrases and images (a season, a place, a texture) over verb-object slogans; 6–15 characters split across 2–3 stacked lines.
